OddsPortal Scraper Node

Enterprise-grade, high-concurrency web scraper utilizing Playwright and Stealth modules to extract historical betting odds.

Parameters:
- targetUrl (String): The URL of the OddsPortal league or match to scrape.
- scrapeAllSeasons (Boolean): Scrape all historical seasons (Not fully implemented yet).
- autoSaveCsvPath (String): Auto-save intermediate batches to CSV.
- autoSaveBatchSize (String): The row count threshold for auto-saving.

Schema Output: Yields a vast matrix of betting odds, match statuses, and scorelines.
